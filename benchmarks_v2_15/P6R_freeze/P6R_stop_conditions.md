# P6R stop conditions

Stop and preserve evidence if a protected/raw hash changes; a manifest fails; FE/PETSc/Abaqus/COMSOL execution becomes necessary; a threshold or projection must change; M2 is synthesized from output; M3 uses the superset hash or event metadata; a detector reads a ground-truth label; fresh-process projections disagree; a baseline success is rhetorically downgraded; or the study is called full P6, held out, untouched or a detection-rate experiment. Do not add cases, strengths or methods after inspection.
