## Historical versus final statuses

Records preserve their original dates and intermediate states. A selection file
may say awaiting receipt, and the candidate ledger may say Click native review
pending. The later binding adjudication is final for the sealed study. The
rejected Click contract is included to explain the HOLD, not to invite native
execution. Trio is a provenance hold, not method failure or successful validation.

The source screening relied on saved connector responses. The public package
includes factual identifiers and closure fields, plus original response hashes;
it does not redistribute third-party issue comments or user-profile collections.
Issue URLs permit independent source inspection, subject to upstream changes.

## Fixed execution protocol, not a new seed experiment

The historical cohort order and timing procedure are in
`retained/contracts/COMPARISON_SEMANTICS.md`. Observations follow a fixed order;
each arm had two evaluation processes per lineage. Saved native process records
retain `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`; host executable-search PATH strings are redacted. The archive does not invent
random seeds or add bootstrap/statistical procedures. Publishing the records does
not change the inference unit or strengthen the paper's claims.
